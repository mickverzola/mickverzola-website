# ADR-001 — Keep a personal portfolio with retained identity

**Status:** Accepted direction; content overhaul not implemented.

**Date:** 2026-10-13. **Decision source:** Owner interview.

## Context

Mick wants a simple personal portfolio, potentially useful to recruiters and employers, that also serves as his side-project gateway. He is open to an overhaul and wants visitors to see a multi-skilled person.

## Decision

Highlight experience, current people leadership at Netflix and selected side projects. Do not foreground a formal job title. Preserve “Finding clarity where others see complexity.”, the M logo, current palette, social links and employment timeline. Retain the Non-Profits presentation as a sample output from the local AI stack.

The ecommerce project and its repository are unpublished. The photos are placeholders whose appearance is liked; their permanent role, final arrangement and GenMedia's future role remain open. Do not infer project facts or destinations.

## Rationale and alternatives

These are the owner's stated preferences. A business application or operating the linked services inside this site would exceed the stated portfolio purpose. A redesign that replaces the liked brand identity would contradict the accepted direction.

## Consequences and verification

Future design work must reconcile professional evidence with the retained identity. Review the preview with Mick for breadth of skills, tone, timeline, socials and sample framing. This record authorizes documenting that direction, not claiming a redesign has shipped.
