Addressing AI Concerns

Human Expertise Comes First

An Assistant, Not an Author

Handles tedious, repetitive tasks (sorting transcripts, synthesizing notes) while experts handle high-level analysis and strategy.

Preserving Integrity

AI outputs are starting points for human review, not final products. Domain experts remain the ultimate authority.

Protecting IP & Values

Hybrid and Local deployment options keep proprietary research strictly confidential and behind your firewall.

Three Ways to Deploy AI

Strategic Model Comparison

Factor Cloud-Based AI Hybrid Local-Cloud AI Fully Local AI

Maintenance Zero IT maintenance Low to moderate IT overhead Requires dedicated IT management
Privacy Standard enterprise cloud APIs High control; local sensitivity filtering 100% offline & air-gapped sovereignty
Primary Advantage Immediate access to top-tier models with zero setup Optimal balance of data safety, scaling, and efficiency Total confidentiality, zero external dependencies

Cloud-Based AI

Cost: Low upfront; predictable subscription per user.
Maintenance: Zero IT maintenance; provider managed.
Privacy: Standard enterprise cloud APIs.
Primary Advantage: Immediate access to top-tier models with zero setup.

Hybrid Local-Cloud AI

Cost: Balanced; low baseline running costs + cloud scale.
Maintenance: Low to moderate IT overhead.
Privacy: High control; sensitive data filtered locally first.
Primary Advantage: Optimal balance of data safety, scaling, and efficiency.

Fully Local AI

Cost: Higher hardware upfront; zero recurring subscription fees.
Maintenance: Requires dedicated internal or partner IT management.
Privacy: Maximum data sovereignty; 100% offline & air-gapped.
Primary Advantage: Total confidentiality, zero external dependencies.

2. Audit Data

Review organizational workflows and categorize data into Public, Internal, and Confidential.

3. Choose Model

Select the Cloud, Hybrid, or Local deployment model that aligns with your privacy profile.
